What We Do

More Than a Filter Supplier

Aven Air Group works with facility teams to understand the problem, identify the right filtration solution and make procurement as simple as possible. From routine HVAC replacement filters to HEPA, molecular filtration, activated carbon and custom applications, we combine responsive service, competitive pricing and reliable supply with direct access to experienced manufacturing and technical resources.

Our customers range from K–12 school districts to public agencies, resorts, data centers, treatment plants and production floors. Indoor air quality shapes how people learn, heal and work, and we treat every filter list as part of that job.

Executive Leadership

Matt Graham

President & Co-Founder

Matt Graham is President and Co-Founder of Aven Air Group, bringing more than two decades of experience in strategic sales leadership, business development and national account management.

Throughout his career, Matt has held senior leadership positions with organizations including R-Zero, Venus Group, FleetLogix, Dickies, GCA Services Group (ABM) and Cintas. His experience spans commercial air filtration, indoor air quality, facilities management, healthcare, education, hospitality and industrial markets.

Matt has developed multimillion-dollar customer relationships, managed a $75 million national account portfolio and built strategic partnerships with major public and private organizations.

At Aven Air Group, Matt leads strategic growth initiatives, manufacturing partnerships and customer development, helping organizations improve indoor air quality, simplify procurement and achieve long-term operational savings.
